## Bahasa Indonesia

Basis data terbuka dan portal direktori interaktif untuk seluruh program studi, fakultas akademik, akreditasi nasional dan internasional, alokasi daya tampung (SNBP, SNBT, UM UGM CBT), kurikulum inti, serta prospek karir lulusan di **Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM)**, Yogyakarta.

Direktori ini dikembangkan bagi calon mahasiswa baru, sivitas akademika, guru bimbingan konseling, serta peneliti pendidikan tinggi untuk mengeksplorasi ekosistem akademik sains, rekayasa, kedokteran, agro-kompleks, sosial humaniora, dan pendidikan vokasi terapan di kampus kerakyatan Bulaksumur.

### Fitur Utama

- **Pencarian Cepat:** Pencarian cerdas secara instan berdasarkan nama program studi, kode prodi, singkatan fakultas, kata kunci kurikulum, maupun karir lulusan.
- **Dukungan Dwibahasa Penuh:** Beralih instan antara Bahasa Indonesia dan English dengan persistensi pengaturan lokal.
- **Filter Multi-Kategori Komprehensif:**
  - **Jenjang:** Sarjana (S1), Sarjana Terapan Vokasi (D4), dan Magister (S2).
  - **Fakultas & Sekolah (19 Unit):** 18 Fakultas Akademik dan Sekolah Vokasi (SV).
  - **Rumpun Keilmuan:** Sains & Teknologi (Saintek), Sosial Humaniora (Soshum), Agro-Kompleks, dan Kesehatan & Kedokteran (Medika).
  - **Program Internasional (IUP):** Penyaringan khusus program studi berkelas International Undergraduate Program.
  - **Akreditasi Global:** AACSB (FEB UGM), ASIIN, ABET, RSC, IABEE, dan AUN-QA.
- **Tinjauan Profil Interaktif (Modal View):**
  - Rincian kuota daya tampung: Total kursi, SNBP Prestasi (30%), SNBT UTBK (30%), dan UM UGM CBT (40%).
  - Deskripsi program studi dan fokus keilmuan inti dwibahasa.
  - Lintasan karir alumni di sektor industri, layanan publik, lembaga riset, dan kewirausahaan.
  - Skema Uang Kuliah Tunggal (UKT Pendidikan Unggul Bersubsidi 100%, 75%, 50%, 25%, dan UKT Unggul Penuh).
  - Tautan ke website resmi program studi dan tombol salin tautan permanen (permalink sharing).
- **Deep Linking:** Buka profil program studi tertentu langsung melalui parameter URL (`?prodi={id}`).
- **Mode Tampilan:** Pilihan tampilan visual kartu (Grid View) dan tabel ringkas (Table View) dengan pengurutan kolom.
- **Open Data & Zero Dependencies:** File data murni JSON, JS, CSV, dan HTML mandiri tanpa ketergantungan runtime eksternal.

### Jalur Penerimaan Mahasiswa Baru

- **SNBP (Seleksi Nasional Berdasarkan Prestasi):** Seleksi nasional tanpa tes berdasarkan penelusuran prestasi akademik rapor dan portofolio (kuota 30%).
- **SNBT (Seleksi Nasional Berdasarkan Tes):** Seleksi nasional ujian tulis berbasis komputer / UTBK serentak se-Indonesia (kuota 30%).
- **UM UGM CBT (Ujian Mandiri Computer Based Test UGM):** Jalur ujian mandiri berbasis komputer yang diselenggarakan oleh UGM di Yogyakarta dan beberapa kota mitra (kuota 40%).
- **IUP (International Undergraduate Program):** Seleksi kelas internasional berbahasa Inggris dengan asesmen Gadjah Mada English Test (GMST), wawancara, dan fokus mobilitas global.

### Kebijakan Uang Kuliah Tunggal (UKT Pendidikan Unggul)

Universitas Gadjah Mada menerapkan kebijakan pembiayaan berkeadilan sosial melalui **UKT Pendidikan Unggul**:
- **UKT Pendidikan Unggul Bersubsidi 100%:** Bebas biaya kuliah (Rp 0 per semester).
- **UKT Pendidikan Unggul Bersubsidi 75%:** Mahasiswa hanya membayar 25% dari biaya operasional pendidikan.
- **UKT Pendidikan Unggul Bersubsidi 50%:** Mahasiswa membayar 50% dari biaya operasional pendidikan.
- **UKT Pendidikan Unggul Bersubsidi 25%:** Mahasiswa membayar 75% dari biaya operasional pendidikan.
- **UKT Pendidikan Unggul (Penuh):** Diberikan bagi mahasiswa dengan kemampuan ekonomi mapan. Calon mahasiswa jalur UM UGM CBT dengan kategori UKT Unggul dikenakan Iuran Pengembangan Institusi (IPI).

## English

An open dataset and interactive web directory cataloging all study programs, academic faculties, national and international accreditations, seat capacity allocations (SNBP, SNBT, UM UGM CBT), core curricula, and graduate career pathways at **Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM)**, Yogyakarta, Indonesia.

Designed for prospective university applicants, academic researchers, guidance counselors, and students to systematically explore Indonesia's premier public university campus in Bulaksumur, spanning natural sciences, engineering, medicine, agriculture, humanities, and applied vocational sciences.

### Key Features

- **Instant Search:** Search across program titles, codes, faculty abbreviations, curriculum topics, and graduate career paths.
- **Complete Bilingual Interface:** Toggle instantly between Bahasa Indonesia and English with client-side persistence.
- **Multi-Category Filtering:**
  - **Degree Level:** Undergraduate (S1), Applied Bachelor / Vocational (D4), and Master's (S2).
  - **Faculty & School (19 Units):** 18 Academic Faculties and Sekolah Vokasi (SV).
  - **Academic Cluster:** Science & Technology, Social Sciences & Humanities, Agro-Complex, and Health Sciences & Medicine.
  - **International Class (IUP):** Filter programs offering international undergraduate curriculum tracks.
  - **Global Accreditations:** AACSB (FEB UGM), ASIIN, ABET, RSC, IABEE, and AUN-QA.
- **Detailed Program Profile Modal:**
  - Exact seat capacity breakdown: Total seats, SNBP (30%), SNBT (30%), and UM UGM CBT (40%).
  - Scientific overview, key learning outcomes, and core curriculum competencies in both languages.
  - Graduate career trajectories across multinational industries, civil service, research institutes, and entrepreneurship.
  - Tuition fee reference (UKT Pendidikan Unggul with 100%, 75%, 50%, 25% subsidy tiers, and full tuition).
  - Direct links to official study program web portals and permalink sharing.
- **Deep Linking:** Access any study program profile directly via URL query parameter (`?prodi={id}`).
- **Dual Display Modes:** Flexible Grid View and sortable compact Table View.
- **Open Data Formats:** Available in JSON, JS, and CSV without external runtime dependencies.

### Admission Pathways

- **SNBP (National Selection Based on Achievement):** National portfolio and high school report card selection (30% quota).
- **SNBT (National Selection Based on Tests):** National standardized computer-based examination / UTBK (30% quota).
- **UM UGM CBT (UGM Computer-Based Examination):** Institutional computer-based entrance examination administered across Yogyakarta and test center cities (40% quota).
- **IUP (International Undergraduate Program):** English-taught international track with specialized entrance testing (GMST) and global exchange requirements.

### Tuition Fees & Equity Subsidy System

UGM applies the socially equitable **UKT Pendidikan Unggul** tuition structure:
- **UKT Pendidikan Unggul Subsidized 100%:** Rp 0 per semester (full scholarship subsidy).
- **UKT Pendidikan Unggul Subsidized 75%:** Student pays 25% of educational operating costs.
- **UKT Pendidikan Unggul Subsidized 50%:** Student pays 50% of educational operating costs.
- **UKT Pendidikan Unggul Subsidized 25%:** Student pays 75% of educational operating costs.
- **UKT Pendidikan Unggul (Full):** Standard unsubsidized fee for capable income brackets. UM UGM CBT entrants placed in this tier contribute to the Institutional Development Fee (IPI).
